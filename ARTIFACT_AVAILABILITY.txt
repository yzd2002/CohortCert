Artifact availability for the Regular Research submission

The public supplement provides analysis scripts and fixed public results for the manuscript tables and six figures; original method, control and audit sources; and the fixed inputs required by the principal CivilComments/GoEmotions CPU replay routes. Each archive has a SHA-256 manifest and a limited-scope integrity entry point. The exact manuscript PDF and component identities are recorded in PAPER_ARTIFACT_BINDING.json.

The current MAST presentation uses the single admitted amended audit, preserving all 56 paths and its original audit history. Four censored paths remain unknown; two scientific-error histories remain uncredited even though their assertions are correct. No method trajectory was rerun for the amendment or the packaging task.

Full Native execution on a new environment, fresh model inference and complete reproduction of every historical experiment have not been validated. External code, model and provider-data requirements and historical raw-trace omissions are documented rather than represented as bundled or successfully rerun. These materials should not be described as EAB-complete.

The actual public URL must be added only after successful publication and an unauthenticated access check. A local path or a planned repository address is not a public supplement URL.
